Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data we collect through acropolisviewapartment.gr, why we collect it, and what rights you have. We collect as little as possible and use it only to answer your booking inquiry.

Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is the host of Acropolis View Monastiraki, Monastiraki, Athens 105 60, Greece. You can reach us at +30 697 696 3694 or by replying to any email you receive from us.

What we collect

When you send an inquiry through the contact form, we collect:

  • your full name and email address;
  • your requested check-in and check-out dates and number of guests;
  • the message you write (optional);
  • the date and time of the inquiry and a random identifier (see “Data stored on your device”), used only to prevent spam.

Please do not include sensitive information (such as ID or payment details) in the message field.

Why we use it

We use your data only to reply to your inquiry, check availability, and arrange your stay. The legal basis is taking steps at your request before entering into a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). We also use the random identifier and timestamp to protect the form against abuse and spam, which is our legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

We never sell your data, and we do not use it for marketing or newsletters.

If you book

If your inquiry leads to a booking, Greek law requires us to collect identification details for every guest (tax ID (Α.Φ.Μ.) for Greek guests, passport number for international guests). These are requested separately, not through this website, and are kept for as long as the law requires.

Who processes your data

The inquiry form is handled by Google services (Google Apps Script, Google Sheets and Gmail), provided by Google, which stores the inquiry and delivers it to us by email. You also receive an automatic confirmation email. Google may process data outside the European Union; such transfers are covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and Google’s standard contractual clauses.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which may log technical data such as your IP address for security and operational purposes.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry data only for as long as needed to respond to you and manage any resulting stay, and then delete it, unless we are required by law to keep it longer.

Data stored on your device

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. When you submit the form, a random identifier (for example “usr_k3j9x2a1b”) is saved in your browser’s local storage. It contains no personal information and is used only to stop the form from being submitted repeatedly within a few seconds. You can delete it at any time by clearing your browser’s site data.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • have it corrected or deleted;
  • restrict or object to its processing;
  • receive it in a portable format.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (www.dpa.gr).

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page, with the date of the last update shown at the top.
