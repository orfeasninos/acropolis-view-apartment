The Property

A Penthouse Above the Historic Center

A cozy, quiet, and bright penthouse apartment on the 7th floor of a commercial building on Agiou Markou Street, with a breathtaking, unobstructed panoramic view of the Acropolis, Lycabettus Hill, the Observatory, and the neoclassical rooftops of Plaka.

  • Walkable Historic Center

    Within walking distance of the Acropolis, Plaka, Psyri, Kolonaki, and Ermou shopping street.

  • Private Rooftop Terrace & Jacuzzi

    Sun beds and a hot tub available year-round, with the lit-up Acropolis in view after dark.

  • Steps From Three Metro Lines

    Close to Monastiraki, Syntagma, and Omonoia stations, plus the city's best restaurants and bars.
